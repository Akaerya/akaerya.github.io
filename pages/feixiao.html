"'The Merlin's Claw' is what's written in the books, and 'The Great General' is what people call me. The former sounds too formal, the latter too exaggerated. Neither suits me. I prefer my own title, the 'Lacking General' — lacking in worries, regrets, and rivals."

Character Summary

Feixiao functions as a Single-Target DPS that utilizes primarily Follow-up Attacks to deal damage. She does not use energy to activate her ultimate; instead she collects Flying Aureus when she and her allies attack.

Build Notes

Recommended CR/CD values are out of combat. 4pc Valorous and 2pc Duran is assumed.

Feixiao gains 36% CD in her Ultimate, and her teammates (Robin, Moze, and/or March 7th [Hunt]) all provide various amounts of CD. As a result, CR substats are generally more valuable than CD substats until you reach 100% CR.

For Eagle builds with Robin, aim for 135 SPD (3 actions in a Concerto with 2 ults) or 112.5+ SPD (3 ults, better with AS or E2 Feixiao).

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

I Venture Forth To Hunt BiS
Her light cone gives her Ultimate DMG a whopping 54% DEF penetration at S1, which is a hefty chunk of damage that can be scaled much higher. Considering most of Feixiao's best supports also sport DEF penetration effects, this can hit close to 100% extremely easily which translates to effectively 2.1x your normal damage when these conditions are met. At base level, this increases Ultimate damage by approximately 35-40%.
Baptism of Pure Thought Alternative
Assuming 3 debuffs to activate the light cone effect, it is a comparable substitute to her signature light cone as it gives DMG% and additionally DEF penetration to FuA and Ultimate DMG. It is quite easy to achieve the 3 debuff effect from her teammates.
See You At the End S5 Alternative
Generic BP option with CRIT DMG and typing DMG% for FuA.
Cruising in the Stellar Sea Alternative
Stat stick with CRIT RATE. As long as you can consistently keep the secondary ATK% active, then it is an okay light cone for Feixiao. The conditional bonus CRIT RATE will mess with gear optimization as you need to decide whether the CRIT should be considered when checking stats.

Relic Sets

The Wind-Soaring Valorous BiS
Feixiao's damage profile comes from her ultimate primarily. Feixiao's Talent FuA can trigger the 4pc consistently so this is her best option.
The Ashblazing Grand Duke Alternative
Comparable to her best set, this is because her damage profile is mainly FuA. This causes the 2pc of this set to be useful and the 4pc is very easy to stack due to hitting multiple times with the ultimate.
Eagle of Twilight Line Alternative
Playing on how Feixiao can quickly acquire and launch two ultimates back-to-back, Eagle's 4pc is extremely strong if you can take advantage of Feixiao taking a lot of turns with a team that can very quickly generate Flying Aureus for her. This set is only recommended if you know how to play for maximizing ultimate usage and if you are missing damage, it is recommended to go one of the other sets instead.

Planar Ornaments

Duran, Dynasty of Running Wolves BiS
Best planar option for FuA DPS due to how easy it is to stack in their typical teams and the steroid it gives once it triggers. A 25% FuA DMG and 25% CRIT DMG bonus is extremely good for a set that pretty much has no drawback for her.
Izumo Gensei and Takama Divine Realm Alternative
An alternative, this assumes that one of your teammates is also going to be a Hunt unit. This is quite easily achieved as the normal units you would pick to be supports in this team would be Moze/Hunt March/Ashveil/Topaz.
Inert Salsotto Alternative
Contains stats Feixiao would like, nothing special about this set.

Character Summary

Castorice is an amazing jack-of-all-trades DPS, boasting commendable AoE damage and Single-Target damage all in one kit. She scales with maximum HP and doesn't consume SP which allows her teams to be much more greedy with skill spamming. However, the drawback is that she requires her team to constantly heal and lose HP in order to charge her unique resource, Newbud, without which she cannot use her ultimate where the bulk of her power lies.

Build Notes

Recommended stats include the Poet 4pc buff, Bone Collection 2pc buff and assume CD body. For a team with RMC + 4pc Poet, aim for 53-58% CR out of combat. The HP recommended values are for 2 HP main stats (DMG orb) and 3 HP main stats respectively (HP orb). There are some cases where 4 HP main stats may be more optimal, but it is highly variable.

Time Waits for No One is used as a HP stat stick for Castorice and provides no additional effects to her.

Investment Recommendation

Castorice's highest priority investment is obtaining Hyacine at E0, followed by Hyacine's S1 light cone. For Castorice's own Eidolons, E1 provides a moderate ~15% boost while her signature light cone (S1) offers a more significant ~30% improvement. E2 is a massive power spike at 70%+ increase, making it the premium investment target for those looking to maximize her potential.

Build Notes

Recommended stats are out of combat. If you're using 4pc Amphoreus on Cyrene, you can aim for 172 SPD out of combat instead with more CR/CD, but it means that you won't get any DMG boost from Cyrene's A6 until her Ultimate is activated. 180+ SPD out of combat is harder to achieve without any SPD-increasing relic sets, so you can sacrifice some CR/CD if you prefer to have the DMG boost. Both result in about the same performance in Evernight or Castorice teams.

For non-Evernight or Castorice teams, it's better to sacrifice CR/CD for more SPD or wear Vonwacq for her to take 3 actions in the first cycle if needed. With 2pc SPD/2pc SPD and Vonwacq, you need 9 SPD subs to hit 180 SPD with Herta LC S5 and 7 SPD subs with S1.

Investment Recommendation

Cyrene's investment value varies depending on the team she's used in. Her E1 provides less than 10% improvement across all teams, making it a low-priority pull. Her signature light cone (S1) is the most consistent investment at 12-20% improvement plus quality-of-life benefits — it's valued at ★★★ in mono-Remembrance teams and ★★★★ in other Chrysos Heir teams. E2 ranges from a moderate ★★ in mono-Remembrance setups (14-20%) to ★★★★ or higher in other team compositions where she benefits more from the extra Eidolon effects.

Build Notes

With her S1, Hysilens needs 129 SPD out of combat (not including RM's SPD buff) to take 2 turns in the 0th cycle in the first wave, but she only needs 123.14+ SPD in the second wave.

For higher investment builds (30+ substats), you can aim for 160+ SPD while maintaining 3300+ ATK. 133.34+ SPD (with ATK boots) builds are slightly better at lower investment. Aim for 152.5+ SPD out of combat if you are using S1 to get 4 turns from cycles 0-1 in the first wave.

Investment Recommendation

Hysilens's most important investment is pulling Kafka E0, as Kafka is essential for detonating the DoTs Hysilens applies. Without Kafka, Hysilens cannot compete with other top-tier DPS units. Her E1 is an exceptional Eidolon, providing a massive 40-65%+ improvement — one of the strongest E1s in the game. Her signature light cone (S1) is a strong but secondary investment at 15-30%+ improvement with quality-of-life SPD benefits. If you must choose between E1 and S1, E1 is the higher priority pick.

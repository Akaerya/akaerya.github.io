Character Summary

Build Notes

Recommended stats are out of combat, but you can decrease CD/SPD recommendations by 30% CD and 6-7 SPD respectively if you are on Hero 4pc. Recommended stats here assume ATK rope on Aglaea as well, but Aglaea teams with Sunday only will still need ERR rope.

Speed recommendations listed here are heavily dependent on your supports. With Sunday only or Cyrene/RMC, use SPD boots and aim for 133.34/143.86+ SPD. With Cyrene/Sunday or E1 Aglaea, use base SPD.

Aglaea gains lots of CD from her teammates (Sunday, RMC, Robin) and her S1 provides up to 54% CD. As a result, CR substats are generally more valuable than CD substats until you reach 100% CR in combat. Sunday also provides 20% CR, while RMC provides 10% CR.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Time Woven Into Gold BiS
Signature light cone, it is the clear BiS for Aglaea as it gives her everything she needs with increased CRIT DMG, increased Basic ATK DMG, and base SPD (which helps in reaching higher SPD thresholds).
Sweat Now, Cry Less S1 - S5 Alternative
Generic BP option for Aglaea as it gives her CRIT and DMG.
Reminiscence S5 Alternative
For Aglaea, as long as she can keep her Garmentmaker on the field, she is able to maintain full stacks of Reminiscence for the entire fight. Players without sufficient energy regen may find that their performance with this cone is worse.
Geniuses' Greetings S5 Alternative
Due to how many turns Aglaea takes between each ultimate activation, the secondary buff may only be active for the first few attacks.

Relic Sets

Hero of Triumphant Song BiS
Best set for Aglaea, this offers her and Garmentmaker ATK and CRIT DMG, both stats that she needs to scale and deal damage.
2pc CRIT% sets / 2pc SPD% sets Alternative
If you do not have a good 4pc Hero of Triumphant Song set, it is sometimes better to mix and match a combination of either 2x CRIT% 2pc sets, or 1x CRIT% 2pc set and 1x SPD% 2pc set, or 2x SPD% 2pc sets.

Planar Ornaments

The Wondrous BananAmusement Park BiS
This set has the best performance on Aglaea out of the options available. However, due to Garmentmaker being a Memosprite, the 32% CRIT DMG bonus doesn't affect it which is fine since Aglaea herself deals a lot of the damage too.
Rutilant Arena Alternative
Again, has the same problem with BananAmusement Park, due to the generic DMG% bonus not affecting the memosprite.
Firmament Frontline: Glamoth Alternative
The 160 SPD requirement is extremely easy to hit for Aglaea, but again like with all the other options, the DMG% does not affect her memosprite.
Arcadia of Woven Dreams Alternative
A set in which the bonuses actually do affect Garmentmaker, however it is a set that locks the team to using as many entities as possible in order to beat out BananAmusement. This can be done if you go for the Aglaea/Cyrene/RMC/Hyacine team composition (forgoing the use of Sunday and using Cyrene as the pseudo-energy buffer).

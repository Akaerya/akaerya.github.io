Description

"Recommended stats assume 4 ATK main stats (body, boots, orb, rope). You can aim for a higher SPD or ERR rope build to increase Cerydra's charge rate, but its value is conditional and better suited for Anaxa teams.

For Phainon teams, Cerydra should be faster than Phainon/Sunday. It is less of a need for Anaxa teams, but it can make the SP consumption of the rotation more comfortable.

If you are using an advancer with Cerydra like Sunday or Bronya, you can either increase your advancer's speed by 20 or decrease your DPS' speed by 20 and use ATK boots instead.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Epoch Etched in Golden Blood BiS
Provides everything Cerydra requires as a buffer for a specific niche of DPS carries. Provides ATK% to further boost Military Merit, restores skill points when dealing Ultimate DMG, and improves the Skill DMG of her allied targets.
The Forever Victual Budget
Free light cone that provides her with a decent ATK bonus provided that she is able to stack all three stacks (she starts combat with 1 from her technique and requires 2 skill uses to fully max this bonus).
Dance! Dance! Dance! Alternative
Generally not recommended due to the low ATK this cone provides and how infrequent Cerydra usually casts her ultimate. This can provide another turn if you meet the damage threshold requirement of the fight without needing a specialized option.

Relic Sets

Sacerdos' Relived Ordeal BiS
She is able to stack and maintain the 4pc bonus with her skill. This is the best support set to further boost CRIT-based unit's DPS.
Any variation of 2pc ATK%/2pc SPD% setsAlternative
This is mainly to help hit SPD breakpoints if you cannot hit it with Sacerdos.
Eagle of Twilight Lane Alternative
The use of this is usually in conjunction with DDD to get more turns in order to spam the skill and get your ultimate back faster. However, this is very SP heavy and not recommended in most teams because it lacks support bonuses and can cause your team to miss out on critical damage breakpoints.

Planar Ornaments

Lushaka, the Sunken Seas BiS
As expected of her hyper-carry supporting kit, Lushaka is the best planar to use as it provides a bonus to a singular target while also providing energy regeneration to Cerydra.
Penacony, the Land of Dreams Alternative
This should only be used with Anaxa and if you already pieces laying around, otherwise don't waste your time with farming or using fragments for this set and use Lushaka instead.
Broken Keel Alternative
Provides a Crit DMG bonus to all allies provided she also has 30% Effect Res. Do not use this set if you don't hit or are sacrificing substats to hit the Effect Res requirement.
Fleet of the Ageless Alternative
Lushaka-lite, it gives the entire team an ATK% boost but it lacks the energy regeneration and it also provides less of a boost than simply using Lushaka.

Character Traces
